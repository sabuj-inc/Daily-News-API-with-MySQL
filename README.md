Features of Daily News website:

1.Trending News Section(Highlight the most popular or frequently read news stories in real time)

2.Categorized News (Business, Entertainment, Health, Science, Sports, Technology)

3.Search Functionality (Allow users to search for specific news articles or topics using keywords.)

4.Display local weather

5.News share on social media(Provide options for sharing articles directly on platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn)

6.Realtime internet connection check(Notify users of their internet connection status)

7.User Registration & Profiles (Allow users to sign up, log in, and manage their profiles.)

8.Allow users to save articles for reading later(Offer a bookmarking feature to save favorite articles for later access)

9.Search History(Allow users to view their previous searches for easy navigation)

10.User Feedback option(Provide a form or option for users to share feedback on the website’s functionality and content)

11.Contact us section (Contact information and a form for user inquiries.)
